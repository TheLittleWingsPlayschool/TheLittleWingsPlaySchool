Our Philosophy & Story

At The Little Wings, we believe every child is born with unique wings of talent. Our mission is to give them a safe sky to test those wings and soar.

Established with the vision of offering high-standard preschooling in Vuyyuru, The Little Wings Play School integrates modern early-childhood methodologies with emotional care. We place safety, sanitation, and child happiness at the center of everything we do.

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers for parents looking for a preschool, play school, nursery, LKG or UKG in Vuyyuru.

What age groups can join The Little Wings Play School in Vuyyuru?

We admit children from 1.5 to 6 years: Toddlers Wing (1.5–3 years), Nursery (3–4 years), Lower Kindergarten or LKG (4–5 years), and Upper Kindergarten or UKG (5–6 years).

Where is The Little Wings Play School in Vuyyuru?

We are on the 1st Floor, DBR Kota Complex, AG & SG College Road, Vuyyuru, Andhra Pradesh 521165. The Plus Code is 9R6R+QG. You can open the location in Google Maps.

Do you offer nursery, LKG and UKG in Vuyyuru?

Yes. Along with our Toddlers play group, we offer Nursery, Lower Kindergarten (LKG) and Upper Kindergarten (UKG) at our Vuyyuru campus.

What are the school timings?

Monday to Friday 9:00 AM to 3:30 PM, and Saturday 9:00 AM to 12:30 PM. Sunday is a holiday.

How do I apply for admissions for 2026–27?

Fill in the enquiry form on this page, call or WhatsApp +91 79955 54105, or visit the campus. After a walkthrough and a short play consultation, you will need the child's birth certificate and immunisation records.

Is the play school campus in Vuyyuru safe?

The campus is CCTV monitored, with supervised entry, and we aim for a 1:10 teacher-child ratio so each child is looked after closely.

Do you provide daycare in Vuyyuru?
